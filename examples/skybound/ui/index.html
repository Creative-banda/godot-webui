How to play

Run, leap and climb five sections to the beacon. Collect light shards on the way, beat the medal times.

Keyboard

  • WASDMove
  • ShiftSprint
  • SpaceJump (hold for height)
  • DragLook around
  • RBack to checkpoint

Touch

  • StickMove; push fully to sprint
  • JumpHold for height
  • SwipeLook anywhere else
  • BackPause
  • Crumbling platforms fall a moment after you land. Keep moving.
  • Pink pads throw you into the sky.
  • The Wing Orb in section 3 unlocks a second jump in mid-air.
